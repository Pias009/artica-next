1Why this matters, in one page

Wholesale buyers decide whether to trust a supplier in the first minute. Before they ask for a price, most of them open the About page to answer three questions: Is this a real company? Can they make my order well? Can I talk to a real person? Real photos and true facts answer these questions. Stock photos and big claims make buyers doubt.

Important: some text on the page today is example text. The current About page has certificates, test standards and numbers (for example "LWG Gold", "BSCI audited", "ISO 9001", "0.4% defect rate", "120+ retail partners") that we wrote as placeholders. If they are not true, they must not stay on a live website: a buyer may ask for the certificate, and a false claim can cost you the order, or worse. In section 3 please tick each one: true (send proof), change (give the correct value), or remove.

Rules for every photo

Please do
  • Shoot in daylight; turn off the flash.
  • Clean and tidy the area first.
  • Hold the phone sideways for landscape shots.
  • Take several photos of each shot; we will pick.
  • Send the original files: email, Google Drive, WeTransfer or Baidu Netdisk.
  • Ask people in the photos for permission.
Please don't
  • Send photos through WeChat or WhatsApp chat (they get compressed). Use "send as file" if you must.
  • Use filters, beauty mode, text, watermarks or collages.
  • Show other brands' logos or customers' private-label products.
  • Use stock photos or photos from other companies.
  • Send screenshots.
