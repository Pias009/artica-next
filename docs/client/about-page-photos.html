About page photos: what to shoot.

The new About page needs real photos of your company so buyers can see you are a real business that makes good boots. A good phone camera is enough. Below is every shot, its shape and size, and how to send them.

For: Owner, Chime Long
From: Website team
15 November 2026

The 6 key shots Must have

A116:9
Showroom or factory floor, wide
Landscape 16:9 · min 2400 × 1350 px

The top of the page. Shoes on showroom shelves, or a wide view of production. Leave empty space on the left for the headline.

A24:5
Owner portrait
Portrait 4:5 · min 1600 × 2000 px

Waist up, smiling at the camera, window light, plain light background. Take 10–20; we pick the best.

A34:3
Materials close-up
Landscape 4:3 · min 2000 × 1500 px

Leather, suede, shearling or soles on a table, very close so the texture is sharp.

A44:3
Cutting and stitching
Landscape 4:3 · min 2000 × 1500 px

Hands cutting leather or sewing an upper. Focus on hands and shoe, not faces.

A54:3
Lasting and soles
Landscape 4:3 · min 2000 × 1500 px

A boot on the last (foot form), or a sole being pressed or attached.

A64:3
Finishing and packing
Landscape 4:3 · min 2000 × 1500 px

Cleaning or boxing finished pairs; boxes going into a 12-pair case is ideal.

Landscape 16:9 · min 2400 × 1350 px

Cartons going into a container, or a loaded, sealed container.

▶16:9
Short video (optional)
Landscape · 1080p or better · 20–40 s

A slow walk through the showroom and production floor. No music needed.

Rules for every photo

Please do
  • Shoot in daylight, flash off.
  • Tidy the area first.
  • Hold the phone sideways for landscape shots.
  • Take several of each shot.
  • Send the original files.
  • Ask people in the photo for permission.
Please don't
  • Send through WeChat or WhatsApp chat (it shrinks photos). Use "send as file".
  • Use filters, beauty mode, text or watermarks.
  • Show other brands' logos or customers' products.
  • Use stock photos or other companies' photos.
How to send

Put all photos in one shared folder (Google Drive, WeTransfer or Baidu Netdisk) and send us the link. Start each file name with its shot number:

A1-showroom-01.jpg
A2-owner-01.jpg
A9-team-01.jpg  ...
The look we want

Bright, calm and clean: white, light blue and warm daylight, with the boots as the hero. Dark, yellow-lit or messy photos won't fit the site.
